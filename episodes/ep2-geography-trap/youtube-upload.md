## Description
Russia and China are two of the biggest countries on Earth, yet both are boxed in by their geography. Russia has no mountains or rivers to guard its western plain and almost no free access to the open ocean. China is surrounded on land and depends on a sea lane that is less than 3 km wide at its narrowest. 🌍

In this documentary we follow the map: the European Plain and the invasion road to Moscow, the Himalayas and the water tower of Asia, the Malacca Dilemma, Taiwan and the First Island Chain, Russia's frozen coast and the Arctic's two doors, and why the United States started the race so far ahead.
